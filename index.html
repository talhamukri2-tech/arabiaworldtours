Before you travel

Questions pilgrims ask.

What is included in an Umrah package?+

Inclusions vary by departure and package. Contact the team for the current flight, visa, hotel, meals, transport, Ziyarat and guidance details before booking.

Do you organise family and group bookings?+

Yes. Arabia World Tours designs journeys for individuals, families and groups. Share your group size and preferred dates for suitable options.

What documents are usually required?+

Requirements can change. The team will provide the current document checklist based on the programme and official rules applicable at booking time.

How early should I enquire?+

Early enquiry is recommended for better planning, especially for Hajj and popular Umrah departure periods.

Can I visit the Pune office?+

Yes. The office is at 916 Synagogue Street, opposite Tandel Masjid, above Status Mens Collection, Pune 411001. Call first to confirm office hours.
